A super easy and fluid bouncing heart
animation in Remotion in less than one
minute. Let's go. Create a new Remotion
project and install the Remotion shapes
library. Render heart component. And to
find the right parameters, use the demo
in our docs and play around with it to
your heart's content. Let's now define
two spring animations. I'm going to call
them brief in and breathe out. When the
half brief is in, the scale becomes
smaller. When it briefs out, it becomes
bigger. Remember that spring animations
go from zero to one which makes it super
easy to do math with them. Once you are
done, don't forget to apply the scale as
a CSS style to the heart. Let's now with
a lot of passion from the bottom of our
heart animate the bottom roundness of
our heart. And to make the animation
look even more natural, we can also make
the cleft of our heart deeper when it
briefes in. Now we can make our heart
transparent and add a stroke when the
brief out animation has not yet started.
And done is our love worthy hearth
animation. Export it in your favorite
video format. And our heart is now ready
for implantation.
